Base64 Encoder / Decoder

Convert text to Base64 or decode Base64 back to plain text.

What is Base64 used for?

Base64 encoding turns text or binary data into a safe text format, commonly used for embedding images in HTML/CSS, sending data in URLs, API tokens, and email attachments. This tool converts between plain text and Base64 in either direction.

How to use it

1. Type or paste your text into the input box.
2. Click "Encode" to convert to Base64, or paste Base64 text and click "Decode" to get plain text back.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Base64 encryption?

No, Base64 is an encoding format, not encryption — it doesn't provide security, just a text-safe representation of data.

Does this work offline?

The tool runs in your browser, so once the page is loaded it doesn't need a server round-trip to encode/decode.
