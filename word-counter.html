Why count words and characters?

Word and character counts matter for essays with strict limits, social media posts (like X's 280-character cap), SEO meta descriptions, resumes, and academic assignments. This tool updates live as you type or paste, so you always know exactly where you stand before you submit or publish.

How to use the Word Counter

1. Paste or type your text into the box above.
2. Word, character, sentence, and paragraph counts update automatically.
3. Edit your text and watch the numbers change in real time — no button clicks needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes, completely free with no signup or download required.

Does it count spaces as characters?

The "Characters" stat includes spaces; "No spaces" shows the count excluding them.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded or saved.
