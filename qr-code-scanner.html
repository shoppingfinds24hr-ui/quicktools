Why use a QR code scanner online?

Sometimes you have a QR code as an image file — a screenshot, a downloaded flyer, or a saved photo — but no phone camera handy, or the code came from a screen rather than a physical print. This tool decodes it right in your browser and shows you the exact link or text it contains, before you click through.

How to use it

1. Click the upload box and choose a QR code image from your device.
2. The link or text hidden inside is decoded automatically.
3. Copy the result, or click "Open Link" to visit it directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No, the image is decoded entirely in your browser — it is never sent anywhere.

What image formats are supported?

Common formats like PNG, JPG, and WEBP all work.
