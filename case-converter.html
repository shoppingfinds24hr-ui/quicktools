Case Converter

Switch text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, and Sentence case.

When do you need a case converter?

Writers, students, and marketers often need to quickly reformat text — turning a shouty all-caps heading into normal sentence case, or fixing a title that wasn't capitalized correctly. Instead of retyping everything, paste it here and convert with one click.

How to use it

1. Paste your text into the box.
2. Click the case style you want.
3. Copy the converted result with the Copy button.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word; Sentence case only capitalizes the first letter of each sentence.

Is there a text length limit?

No practical limit — it works entirely in your browser.
