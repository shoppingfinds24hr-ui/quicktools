Link Generator

Shorten any long URL and copy it, or turn it straight into a QR code.

Or generate a link from text

Paste any note, message, or text below — get a shareable link that shows that exact text when opened.

Why shorten a link?

Long URLs are hard to share, hard to type, and take up more space in a QR code (making it denser and harder to scan). A short link is easier to share on social media, print on business cards or flyers, and turns into a cleaner, faster-scanning QR code.

How to use it

1. Paste your long link into the box above.
2. Click "Generate Short Link" — a shortened version appears.
3. Copy it directly, or click "Generate QR Code" to jump straight to the QR tool with your short link pre-filled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these short links expire?

Short links are created via a third-party shortening service and generally remain active long-term, though this tool does not control their uptime.

Is this useful for QR codes?

Yes — shorter URLs create simpler, more reliable QR codes that scan faster, especially at small print sizes.
