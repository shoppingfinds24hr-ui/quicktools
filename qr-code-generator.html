QR Code Generator

Turn any link or text into a scannable QR code.

What can you use a QR code for?

QR codes are useful for business cards, restaurant menus, product packaging, WiFi sharing, event invites, and payment links. Just type or paste your text or URL above and a scannable code appears instantly — right-click to save it as an image.

How to use it

1. Type or paste your link or text into the box.
2. The QR code generates automatically.
3. Right-click (or long-press on mobile) to save the image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No, the code encodes your text/link directly and works forever — there's no tracking server involved.

Can I generate a QR code for WiFi or contact info?

Yes, paste the appropriately formatted string (e.g. WIFI:S:name;T:WPA;P:password;;) and it will encode correctly.
